--explain--

Division shares an amount into equal groups. 10 grapes shared between 2 friends is $10 \div 2 = 5$ grapes each.

--answer--

15 stickers are shared equally between 3 friends. How many stickers does each friend get?

```yaml
- check: equals
  expected: 5
  reason_code: wrong_quotient
  reason: Hand out the stickers one at a time to each friend until none are left.
```

--answer--

What is $24 \div 4$?

```yaml
- check: equals
  expected: 6
  reason_code: wrong_quotient
  reason: Ask how many fours fit into the first number.
```
