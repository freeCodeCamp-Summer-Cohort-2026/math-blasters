--explain--

Division undoes multiplication. Because $5 \times 3 = 15$, you also know $15 \div 3 = 5$ and $15 \div 5 = 3$. Use the times tables you know to divide.

--answer--

$6 \times 8 = 48$. What is $48 \div 6$?
