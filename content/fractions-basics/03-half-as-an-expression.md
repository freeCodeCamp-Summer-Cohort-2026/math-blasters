--explain--

You can halve an unknown number too. Half of $x$ is written $\frac{x}{2}$.

--answer--

Write half of $x$ as an expression.

```yaml
- check: equivalent
  expected: x/2
  reason_code: not_half
  reason: Halving an unknown works like halving a number, so divide it by two.
```
