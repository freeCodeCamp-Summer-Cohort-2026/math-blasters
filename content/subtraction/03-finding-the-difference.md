--explain--

Subtraction also tells you how far apart two numbers are. Mia has 9 shells and Leo has 6. Mia has $9 - 6 = 3$ more shells than Leo. That gap is called the **difference**.

--answer--

Sam read 11 books this term and Priya read 7. How many more books did Sam read?

```yaml

  reason: Subtract the smaller amount from the bigger one to find the gap.
```
