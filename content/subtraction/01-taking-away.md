--explain--

Subtraction takes one amount away from another. If you have 8 grapes and eat 3, you have $8 - 3 = 5$ grapes left.

--answer--

You have 9 balloons and 4 float away. How many balloons are left?

```yaml
- check: equals
  expected: 5
  reason_code: wrong_difference
  reason: Start with all the balloons, then take away the ones that floated off.
```

--answer--

What is $12 - 5$?

```yaml
- check: equals
  expected: 7
  reason_code: wrong_difference
  reason: Take the second number away from the first. Counting on your fingers helps.
```
